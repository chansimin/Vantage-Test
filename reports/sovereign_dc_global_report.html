Method & caveats

2030 proration method

For a program with an explicit start–end window straddling 2030, the by-2030 value is total × (2030 − start) / (end − start) — a straight-line run-rate, e.g. Saudi Arabia's 6.6GW/2025–2035 implies ~3.3GW by 2030 (5 of 10 years elapsed). This assumes even deployment across the window, which likely understates near-term-heavy build-outs and overstates back-loaded ones — it's a planning-level estimate, not a schedule.

Programs ending at or before 2030, or already-realized single-date announcements, count at full value with no proration. Programs with no disclosed completion date at all are shown at full value but tagged UNCERTAIN — proration was not possible, not assumed unnecessary.

Two independent signals, triangulated

Each country contributes a budget figure and a capacity figure exactly as its government announced them — these are often separate programs, not derived from one another. Where capacity wasn't stated directly: (1) direct MW, (2) GPU count × kW/GPU (1.5kW H100-class, 2.1kW Blackwell-class), (3) budget ÷ $15–38M/MW capex benchmark, in that preference order.

Only counted as "sovereign" if a government body, sovereign wealth fund, or state-owned enterprise is the funder, subsidizer, or explicit sponsor — the ~$500B private "Stargate" JV and similar hyperscaler-only deals are excluded even where politically endorsed.
