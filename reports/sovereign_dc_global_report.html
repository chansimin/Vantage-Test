Method & caveats

Two independent signals, triangulated

Each country contributes a budget figure and a capacity figure exactly as its government announced them — these are often separate programs, not derived from one another (e.g. Saudi Arabia's $100B HUMAIN commitment and its 6.6GW target are both headline figures for the same push, not a computed pair).

Where capacity wasn't stated directly, it's estimated — in order of preference: (1) direct MW, (2) GPU count × a kW/GPU factor (1.5kW for H100-class, 2.1kW for Blackwell-class, both PUE-inclusive), (3) budget ÷ a $/MW capex benchmark ($15–38M/MW depending on whether the spend is infra-only or all-in).

Scope: sovereign ≠ commercial

Only counted if a government body, sovereign wealth fund, or state-owned enterprise is the funder, subsidizer, or explicit sponsor. The ≈$500B private “Stargate” JV, Malaysia's 600MW YTL/Nvidia campus, and similar hyperscaler-only deals are excluded even where politically endorsed.

Japan's figure excludes the ¥10T+/7yr semiconductor-fab umbrella (Rapidus/TSMC subsidies) to keep the comparison to compute/DC spend consistent across countries — including chip-fab policy would inflate Japan's number against a different asset class than everyone else's.

Assessed, no qualifying program found

Researched and explicitly excluded — either no government funding role, or the government's role was limited to tax/permitting facilitation of private capex.
